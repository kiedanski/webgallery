WebGallery is a private, self-hosted photo gallery for Android. It connects to your Tilde personal cloud server over WebDAV and gives you a fast, native gallery experience with offline support.

Features:
- Offline-first thumbnail browsing with efficient incremental sync
- Background sync with progress notification — handles 100k+ photo libraries
- Full-resolution photo viewer with pinch-to-zoom and swipe navigation
- Video streaming with auto-play
- Year timeline for fast navigation
- Mark photos as favorites for permanent offline access
- Flag photos for inspection with error diagnostics
- Change photo dates, edit tags, and delete photos
- Offline mutation queue — edits work without connectivity
- Auto-upload from Camera, WhatsApp, or any folder
- Material 3 UI with dark mode and dynamic colors
- HTTP Basic Auth credentials encrypted with AES-256-GCM
- Zero proprietary dependencies — fully F-Droid compatible
- GPLv3 licensed
